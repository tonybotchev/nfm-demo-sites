The smile makeover, explained honestly.

Invisalign or veneers? They're different tools for different goals. Here's how Dr. Sheth helps you choose — no pressure, just straight answers:

Invisalign® Clear Aligners

Removable, nearly invisible trays that gradually straighten your teeth — no brackets, no wires, no food restrictions.

Why patients love it
  • Virtually invisible treatment
  • Eat and drink normally
  • Fewer office visits than braces
  • Easy to keep teeth clean
Keep in mind
  • Trays must be worn 20–22 hrs/day
  • Not ideal for severe cases
  • Requires discipline

Porcelain Veneers

Ultra-thin porcelain shells bonded to the front of your teeth — the fastest path to a dramatic, camera-ready smile.

Why patients love it
  • Dramatic results in weeks, not months
  • Fixes chips, gaps & stubborn stains
  • Stain-resistant porcelain
  • Natural look, custom shade
Keep in mind
  • Some enamel reshaping required
  • Higher upfront investment
  • Not reversible
