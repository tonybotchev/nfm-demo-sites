Leaks, lifted shingles, wind damage — we find the real source of the problem and fix it once, instead of selling you a roof you don't need yet.

  • Leak tracing and patching
  • Wind and hail damage repair

Chimneys are where most "mystery leaks" start. We repair chimney liners, rebuild flashing, and seal the joints roofers love to skip.

  • Chimney repair
  • Chimney liner repair
  • Flashing replacement and resealing
  • Penetration and vent sealing

STORM & HAIL INSPECTIONS

North Texas hail doesn't always leave obvious damage. We inspect every slope, document what we find, and give you a straight answer on next steps.

  • Full-slope hail and wind inspection
  • Photo documentation of damage
  • Insurance-claim-ready findings
  • Straight talk — no manufactured damage
