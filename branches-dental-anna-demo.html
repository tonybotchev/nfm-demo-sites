Our Story

Why a dentist from Nairobi chose Anna, Texas.

"As a family practice and parents ourselves, we know how important dental health is for you and your loved ones." — The Branches Dental philosophy

Branches Dental isn't a corporate chain. It's the practice Dr. Thakkar dreamed about on his way from Kenya to Milwaukee to Chicago to a small town in Wisconsin — and finally home to Anna, where he's raising his own family.

Along the way he's taught oral health in school programs and cared for elderly patients in nursing homes. That same gentle, unhurried care is what Anna families get every visit.
