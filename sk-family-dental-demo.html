Meet the dentist

Dr. Sachin Kulkarni

"Dr. K" · DDS

Dr. Kulkarni earned both his BA in Economics and his Doctorate of Dental Surgery at the University of Michigan–Ann Arbor, then completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency in Phoenix, Arizona.

After four years practicing in Phoenix, he moved his family to the Dallas area to be closer to relatives — and opened his practice in fast-growing, family-oriented McKinney.

His philosophy: excellence begins with careful diagnosis. He learns your concerns, encourages questions, and builds the treatment plan with you — in a gentle, stress-free environment.
