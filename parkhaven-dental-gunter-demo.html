Dental Services

Preventive, restorative, cosmetic, and emergency care for the whole family — from a child's first checkup to a full smile makeover.

Family Dentistry

Gentle, personalized care for patients of all ages — the team takes time to understand each person's needs, goals, and lifestyle.

  • Checkups & professional cleanings
  • Kid-friendly dentistry
  • Fillings & bonding
  • Dental education for lasting oral health

Cosmetic Dentistry

Enhance the brightness, shape, and size of your teeth — customized to the smile you want, without hindering how your teeth work.

  • Veneers
  • Professional teeth whitening
  • Crowns & bridges
  • Smile makeovers

Emergency Dentistry

Dental pain doesn't wait. When something hurts, call — the team will work to see you and get you comfortable fast.

  • Toothaches & dental pain
  • Root canals
  • Extractions
  • Broken or knocked-out teeth

Meet the dentist

Dr. Bryant Lee, DMD

Doctor of Dental Medicine — Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine

Dr. Lee brings broad clinical experience — from military service and corporate offices to underserved communities and private practice — with clinical interests in general dentistry, root canals, extractions, and cosmetic procedures.

He believes in educating patients and empowering them to make good choices for their oral health — and in meeting with you personally about pain concerns before any procedure.
