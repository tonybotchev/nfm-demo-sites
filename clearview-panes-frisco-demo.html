Our Story

From a Frisco classroom to Frisco homes.

The story that built ClearView Panes — and the reason Frisco homeowners trust us with their homes.

The teacher

Brandy Rock taught in Frisco ISD. She knows this town, its families, and its homes — because she raised her own here.

The mother of five

Brandy and her husband Jason have been married for 24 years and are raising five kids. Family isn't a marketing line for them — it's the whole business model.

The COVID years

Brandy started helping with the office side of the window business during the COVID era — and discovered she was built for this: straight answers, fair dealing, no pressure.

The partner

ClearView Panes is co-owned by Brandy and Jason alongside a trusted friend, a former military veteran — the kind of partner you want on a crew working in your home.
