Holistic Treatments

Care your whole body will thank you for

Therapy 01

Ozone Therapy

Ozone is a naturally occurring molecule long used in biological dentistry to support disinfection and healing. At Easy Dental it is part of a holistic toolkit aimed at treating infection gently — without reaching for the harshest option first.

  • Supports the body’s own healing response
  • A natural approach to disinfection in dental care
  • Ask the team how ozone fits into your treatment plan
Ask About Ozone — (972) 540-0605

Therapy 02

Safe Mercury Filling Removal

Old silver amalgam fillings contain mercury — and removing them the wrong way can expose you to more of it, not less. Easy Dental specializes in the safe removal of mercury fillings, using protocols designed to protect you during the process.

  • Removal protocols designed to minimize mercury exposure
  • A cornerstone of biological dentistry — done right
  • Call to discuss whether your old fillings should be evaluated
Discuss Your Fillings — (972) 540-0605

Therapy 03

Laser Disinfection

Dental lasers allow for precise, minimally invasive treatment — disinfecting treatment areas with remarkable accuracy while supporting gentler healing. It is modern dentistry aligned with a holistic mindset: do only what is needed, as gently as possible.

  • Precise, targeted disinfection during treatment
  • Minimally invasive approach that supports healing
  • Ask whether laser care is right for your visit
Ask About Laser Care — (972) 540-0605
