Braces & Invisalign, without the referral runaround.

Traditional Braces

The workhorse of orthodontics — precise control for complex cases, for kids, teens, and adults alike.

  • Full treatment planned by Dr. Ramoliya
  • All adjustments in-office, no referrals
  • Flexible scheduling, including late Tuesdays

Good to know

Most patients are surprised how fast modern braces move teeth. Your consultation maps the whole timeline — and the whole cost — before anything starts.

Free orthodontic consultation

Clear Braces

Same straightening power as metal braces, with tooth-colored brackets that blend in. A favorite for teens and image-conscious adults.

  • Discreet ceramic brackets
  • Full treatment in one office
  • Evening appointments available

Good to know

Clear braces cost only slightly more than traditional — and most patients say coworkers never notice them.

Free orthodontic consultation

Invisalign® Clear Aligners

Straighten without brackets or wires. Removable trays, no food restrictions, and check-ins that fit your calendar.

  • Nearly invisible treatment
  • Eat whatever you like
  • Fewer office visits than braces

Good to know

Not everyone is a candidate — Dr. Ramoliya will tell you honestly if aligners or braces fit your case better. No pressure either way.
