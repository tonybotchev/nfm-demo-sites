The story

The shop people remember by name.

Dixon Welch Plumbing doesn't need a slogan — the neighbors wrote one. A reviewer calls them the “Melissa TX home town plumber,” and that's exactly how the shop earned it: one remembered name at a time.

Another customer spent three days waiting on a different plumber because they weren't sure who had installed the water heater originally. Then they remembered: it was Dixon. He came out within a few hours and got it taken care of at a very reasonable price.

It's family-owned-and-run, and it shows in the work — fixed fast, done right, and priced fair. That's the whole business model, and it's been working in Melissa for years.

Why it matters: when your plumber is a name, not a dispatch number, you get the person who remembers your house — not the next available truck.
