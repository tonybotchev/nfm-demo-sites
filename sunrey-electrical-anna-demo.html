Why "with a conscience"

"Our decisions are guided by doing what's right — not what our wallet dictates."

SunRey started from nothing and grew organically through hard work, craftsmanship, and word-of-mouth referral. That roots shows up in how the business runs:

  1. Licensed electricians only. Master, journeyman and apprentice electricians fully licensed by the State of Texas — never whoever's available.
  2. Community first. Volunteerism, charitable contributions, fundraisers and pro-bono services in the communities we live and work in.
  3. Homegrown talent. An apprenticeship program that teaches the craft through real mentorship — above industry-standard pay from day one.
  4. Sustainable where it counts. Solar and renewable guidance when it's genuinely the right answer for the customer.
