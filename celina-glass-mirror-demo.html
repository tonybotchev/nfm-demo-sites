What We Do

The full craft of glass & mirror

Tap a craft to see what it covers. Everything below is work we actually do — cut, fitted, and finished by a local shop.

Frameless shower doors

Nothing changes a bathroom like glass does. We measure, cut, and install frameless shower doors and shower glass that make the room feel twice as big — clean lines, no bulky frames, easy to keep spotless.

  • Frameless shower doors
  • Shower glass panels & enclosures
  • Measured & cut for your exact opening

Custom mirrors

Off-the-shelf never fits quite right. We cut mirrors to your exact dimensions — bathroom vanities, statement walls, gyms, and dressing areas — with polished edges and a clean, professional mount.

  • Custom-cut mirrors, any size
  • Vanity & bathroom mirrors
  • Wall & statement mirrors

Window glass

Cracked pane, foggy seal, or a full upgrade — we replace window glass and install energy-efficient replacement windows that cut drafts and keep Celina's summer heat where it belongs: outside.

  • Window glass replacement & repair
  • Energy-efficient replacement windows
  • Storm & impact window glass

Solar & bug screens

Texas sun is relentless. Solar screens block the heat before it hits your glass — lower cooling bills, less glare, and your furniture stops fading. Bug screens keep the outdoors out while the breeze comes in.

  • Solar screens for heat & glare control
  • Bug screens, fitted to your frames
  • A cooler, more comfortable home

Table top glass

Protect the table you love without hiding it. We cut table top glass to your exact shape — round, rectangle, or something unusual — with smooth, safe edges that look like they were always meant to be there.

  • Custom-cut table top glass
  • Any shape, any size
  • Polished, furniture-safe edges
