The honest comparison

Tank vs. tankless — why it matters

Most plumbers will install whatever you ask for. A specialist tells you the difference first.

Traditional tank heater

  • Heats 40–50 gallons around the clock, used or not
  • Runs out — the last shower goes cold
  • Average life: 8–12 years
  • Tank can corrode, leak, and flood
  • Takes up a full closet or garage corner

Tankless (on-demand)

  • Heats water only when a tap opens
  • Endless hot water — never runs out mid-shower
  • Up to 20 years with annual descaling
  • No stored water = no tank-burst flood risk
  • Wall-mounted — reclaims your floor space
