One specialty. Done exceptionally well.

General dentists do a little of everything. Dr. Blacher does one thing — saving teeth through root canal therapy — with the training, technology, and focus of a specialist.

Root Canal Treatment

When infection or injury reaches the inner pulp of your tooth, a root canal removes the damaged tissue, relieves your pain, and lets you keep your natural tooth — usually in a single comfortable visit.

  • Precise, microscope-guided technique for accuracy
  • Profound anesthesia — comfort is the priority, every time
  • Most treatments completed in one appointment
  • Clear aftercare guidance so healing is uneventful
📞 Discuss your tooth: (469) 202-5050

Root Canal Retreatment

Sometimes a tooth that had a root canal years ago starts hurting again. Retreatment gives that tooth a second chance — Dr. Blacher re-enters the tooth, clears persistent infection, and seals it properly.

  • Specialist-level diagnosis of why the first treatment failed
  • Advanced imaging to find hidden canals and fractures
  • A second chance to save your natural tooth
  • Honest guidance if extraction is truly the better option

Meet Dr. Jonathan Blacher

DMD, MSD · Board-Certified Endodontist

A Milwaukee native, Dr. Blacher graduated from the University of Minnesota, earned his dental degree at Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine, then completed his endodontic residency at the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine — earning both his Endodontic Certificate and a Master's in Dental Science.

His pioneering research on mandibular block anesthesia was published in the Journal of Endodontics, with further research featured in the Journal of Dental Education. He stays at the forefront of dental technology so your visit is comfortable and stress-free.

Root canal questions, answered.

Straight answers before you ever pick up the phone.

Endodontists complete 2–3 extra years of specialty training focused entirely on the inside of the tooth — diagnosing tooth pain and performing root canals. It's all they do, all day, which means deeper expertise and usually faster, more comfortable treatment.

With modern anesthesia and technique, most patients report a root canal feels similar to getting a filling. Dr. Blacher's research background is in anesthesia itself — comfort is quite literally his specialty within the specialty.

Most root canal treatments are completed in a single appointment. Complex cases or retreatments can occasionally need a second visit — you'll always know the plan before treatment begins.

Tooth pain is treated as a priority here — patients are regularly seen within 24 hours of calling. Call (469) 202-5050 and describe your symptoms; the team will get you on the schedule.

You'll receive clear aftercare instructions, and most people return to normal activities the same day. Your general dentist will typically place the final crown or restoration to protect the tooth long-term.

In pain right now?

Don't wait it out — tooth pain rarely resolves on its own. Call during office hours (Mon–Fri, 8 AM–5 PM) and the team will get you scheduled, often as soon as the next day.
