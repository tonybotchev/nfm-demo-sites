Storm & Hail Restoration

Hail damage hides. We know where to look.

North Texas hail doesn't always leave an obvious hole — but it shortens your roof's life and weakens your next claim. Our free, no-obligation home evaluation finds what's really there.

  • Bruised or cracked shingles — hail knocks granules loose and fractures the mat underneath, where you can't see it from the ground.
  • Granule loss in gutters — piles of grit after a storm mean your shingles are losing their armor.
  • Dented vents, flashing & gutters — soft metal tells the story of what the shingles took.
  • Dark spots & premature aging — UV eats exposed fiberglass fast; a damaged roof ages years in months.
